FinTrack Productivity Overlay — Phases 1 through 6

Phase 1: Productivity workspace foundation and settings
Phase 2: Tasks, Projects and My Day
Phase 3: Calendar and Eisenhower Matrix
Phase 4: Habits and Focus Timer
Phase 5: Personal Goals, Milestones, Notes and Countdowns
Phase 6: Daily Reviews, Weekly Reviews and Productivity Insights

This package is an overlay for the existing FinTrack project, not a standalone application.
Merge it into the existing ~/fintrack folder. Do not delete or replace the full project.
